Present Perect vs Past
Present Perfect == recent or new
Past == not recent or new
Ex. Mozart was a composer. He wrote more than 600 peices of music.
If the situation now is different, use the simple past
Sally went out to the country for a few days, but she's back now.

3. Present Perfect Continuos
Normally, used for an activity that has recently stopped or just stopped.
But with how long, for , since, the activity is still happening or has just stopped
Ex. It began raining two hours ago, and it still raining -> How long has it benn raining? -> It's been raining for two hours
